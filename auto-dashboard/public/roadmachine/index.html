Color Match

Lime + black frame. Keep gear clean so the yellow stays the star. Quick rule:

White

Coolest in FL heat, sharp contrast, makes the lime pop. Safest stylish pick.

Black

Stealth, matches the frame's black. Goes with everything, hides road grime.

Hi-Viz Lime

Bold: echoes the frame AND boosts road visibility. Best safety play.

Avoid

Red / orange / pink fight with lime yellow. Skip them.

Smart Add-Ons

What's actually worth grabbing for road riding right now. Tip: stack small items at Mack to clear $400 and trigger your $40-off coupon.

Flat kit

Spare tube, CO2 inflator + cartridge, tire levers, mini multi-tool, saddle bag. Non-negotiable for road.

Bottle cages + bottles

Two cages. FL heat = hydrate. Cheap, easy coupon-stackers at Mack.

Front + rear lights

Skip if you get the Giro Escape (built-in). Otherwise a rear blinker minimum for visibility.

Padded bib short

Giordana "Gold Standard" bib — $199 (was $250). Or Mavic clothing up to 60% off.

Cycling sunglasses

Sun + bugs at speed. Mack has a glasses wall; many under $50.

Socks

$10 cycling socks at Mack. Easy filler to hit the coupon threshold.

Floor pump

If you don't own one. Road tires need topping before every ride.

Grease / anti-seize

For the pedal threads (humid FL seizes them). A dab on install. Remember: left pedal is reverse-threaded.
